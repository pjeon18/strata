How to play
Seal the layer

Shoot blocks onto every face of the core. The moment no sky can reach it, the layer seals into the core and scores.

Guard the core

A meteor that hits bare core cracks it. Three cracks end the run, and every seal mends one. Blast meteors before they land.

Dash through

Press Q to dash. You pass straight through meteors and shots while dashing, and a close call gives the charge back.
